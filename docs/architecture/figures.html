Architecture 3 / 5

How every simulation checks itself

A result that fails its own checks is never shown as a pass. Checks that don't apply are listed as skipped, with the reason.

1 · Before solvingUnits parsed and every equation term dimensionally consistent. Boundary conditions complete and non-contradictory; rigid-body motion rejected.
2 · Solve on 3 gridsn/4, n/2, n. Richardson extrapolation gives the observed order and the error left in the answer.
3 · Known solutionsExact answers or benchmarks (Ghia 1982, Hagen-Poiseuille, beam theory, Fresnel), or a manufactured solution.
4 · Physics lawsEnergy, mass, force and moment balance, entropy ≥ 0, Gauss, div B = 0, Poynting.
5 · Math sanitySolver residuals, CFL stability of the time step, symmetry preserved.
ReportPASS · WARN · FAIL · skip for each check, with the numbers behind it. Verdict: VERIFIED, PASSED WITH WARNINGS or FAILED. Tests inject wrong units, unstable steps, missing supports and wrong physics, and require the matching check to fail.
259 automated tests · 32 simulations · CI on every push
Architecture 4 / 5

The optimiser

It never accepts a change on a promise: only a measured improvement counts, and the final design is re-verified in full.

Run every testfast pass, no grid study
Generate candidatesThicker, wider, longer, more fins, bigger battery, other material, tunable inputs
Re-test every candidateKeep the one that fixes most, at least added mass
TrimRemove material while every test keeps ≥ +5% margin
Verify in fullGrid study, known solutions, conservation on the final design
Guard railsDimensions stay within 0.35×–4× of the original. A housing can never shrink below what it holds (a loophole the optimiser found and we closed). A failure no change can fix is reported as needing a new idea.
Radar node: paint 0.6 → 0.24 absorptivity · battery ×1.3 · mast 40 → 80 mm · then trimmed · 5/5 pass
